Privacy Policy for RelayTip

Effective Date: October 17, 2026 | Last Updated: October 17, 2026

1. Overview

RelayTip ("we," "our," or "us") provides a non-custodial browser extension designed to facilitate real-time cryptocurrency micro-tipping and automated revenue splitting. We prioritize user privacy and operate under a strict privacy-first, local-execution model. RelayTip does not maintain centralized user accounts, track your real-world identity, or store your private cryptographic keys.

2. Information We Handle

RelayTip processes minimal data directly on your local device to perform its core single-purpose function (facilitating micro-tips):

  • On-Page Attributes & Page Context: To display tip prompts, RelayTip scans web pages locally for designated DOM attributes (e.g., relaytip tags). This parsing is performed entirely within your browser runtime; no web browsing history or page content is logged, transmitted, or stored on remote servers.
  • Local Storage Data: Your extension configurations, preferred tipping defaults, and locally cached settings are stored in your browser’s local storage (such as Chrome Storage API or local indexed databases).
  • Blockchain Transaction Data: When you initiate a micro-tip, transaction payload requests are sent to your connected Web3 wallet provider (e.g., via EIP-1193 / EIP-6963 window objects). All completed transactions are publicly visible on their respective blockchain networks (e.g., Shibarium, EVM, or other supported chains).

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, physical addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Private Keys or Passphrases: RelayTip never requests, accesses, or stores your seed phrases, private keys, or wallet credentials.
  • No Web Tracking or Analytics Profiling: We do not track your browsing history across non-integrated sites, build behavioral profiles, or use cross-site tracking cookies.

4. Third-Party Services & Blockchain Nodes

To dispatch transactions and retrieve network state (e.g., gas fees, block height), RelayTip interacts with decentralized blockchain RPC endpoints and third-party Web3 wallet extensions. These RPC providers and wallet software operate under their own respective privacy policies.

5. Google Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

RelayTip's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • User data is only used to provide or improve the core user-facing functionality of RelayTip.
  • User data is never transferred, sold, or disclosed to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • User data is never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

6. Security

We rely on industry-standard browser isolation sandboxing and secure encrypted HTTPS/WSS channels for node communications. However, security also depends on your operating system environment and wallet security practices.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect extension updates or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published alongside extension releases with a revised "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or RelayTip operations, please contact us via our official repository or support portal: support@relaytip.io.
